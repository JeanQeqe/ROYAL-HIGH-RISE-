About Royal High Rise Holdings

A Trusted Force in African Commodity Trading

Royal High Rise Holdings is a South African-based commodity trading and logistics company specializing in the sourcing, procurement, and supply of chrome concentrate from Zimbabwe and Southern Africa to verified buyers and industrial off-takers.

We operate at the core of the mining and commodity supply chain sector — with direct access to chrome concentrate suppliers, established buyer networks, and the operational expertise to facilitate reliable and scalable commodity transactions.

Our business is built on trust, transparency, and long-term partnership. Every transaction we facilitate reflects our commitment to professionalism and market integrity across Southern Africa.

Our Mission

Build a trusted, scalable commodity trading operation connecting African minerals to global industrial markets through efficient logistics and transparent transactions.

Our Vision

Become a leading African commodity trading company renowned for reliability, professionalism, and long-term trade partnerships across global markets.

Comprehensive Trading & Logistics Services

End-to-end commodity solutions from mine to market, tailored for buyers, suppliers, investors, and exporters across Southern Africa and global markets.

Commodity Procurement

Mining Logistics

Trade Finance Partnerships
